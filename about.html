About

The Story Behind the Jars

Licorice Whips is a love letter to the corner sweet shop.

We remember the hush of a bell above the door, rows of glass jars and the delicious difficulty of choosing just one. Our shop brings that feeling home, with classic licorice, sparkling gummies, fine chocolates and retro favourites, carefully packed and sent to your door.

Every sweet is chosen to be shared, gifted and savoured.
